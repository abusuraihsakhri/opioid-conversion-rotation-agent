Opioid morphine milligram equivalent (MME) calculator

A local, browser-only reference calculator using the CDC 2022 MME factors for selected outpatient opioid formulations.

Not a prescribing or opioid-rotation tool. MME is an estimate for population-level risk assessment; it is not an equianalgesic replacement dose. Do not use calculated MMEs to switch opioids, determine methadone doses, select fentanyl patches, or direct tapering.

Requires clinical review of indication, opioid tolerance, formulation, concurrent drugs, age, organ function, and individual response. The CDC chronic-pain guideline is not intended as a protocol for cancer-related pain, palliative care, or end-of-life care.

Method and limitations

For oral products: daily dose (mg/day) × CDC factor. For transdermal fentanyl: delivery rate (mcg/hour) × 2.4. Methadone, injectable formulations, and other complex conversions are deliberately excluded from this reference interface.

The result is an estimate, not a dose recommendation, risk category, or prescription. No identifying information is requested; entries remain in this page's volatile memory and are not transmitted to an application server.

Source: CDC Clinical Practice Guideline for Prescribing Opioids for Pain (2022), MME conversion table and cautions.
